## What this is

A proof-of-concept machine learning pipeline that predicts whether a *Wolffia globosa*
(the world's smallest flowering plant) cultivation tank is heading toward fast, healthy
growth or toward stagnation/decline — using only the composition of its frond-associated
bacterial community and a handful of water-chemistry readings, on a **literature-grounded
synthetic dataset**, since no in-house sequenced cohort exists yet.

This repository accompanies a short paper submitted to *BEACON*, the annual magazine of the
Department of Mathematics (13th Edition, "Through the Primal Lens"). The paper's Methodology
and Results sections map directly onto the notebook cells listed below.

## Why this exists (short version)

*Wolffia globosa* is the fastest-doubling flowering plant known and a serious candidate
protein crop, but it has no roots and no vascular reserve to buffer stress — so visible
signs of a failing culture (chlorosis, frond loss) only appear once the culture is already
collapsing, and current cultivation quality control relies on manual inspection and water
chemistry, not on any direct read of the microbial community actually driving growth. This
project asks whether the phytomicrobiome itself can be turned into an earlier, automatable,
and *explainable* warning signal. See the paper for the full argument and citations.

## The pipeline, in one paragraph

A synthetic cohort of 450 simulated tank samples is generated, each carrying twelve
bacterial taxa (five literature-documented plant-growth-promoting species, five
opportunistic/fouling-associated species, two functionally neutral controls) as
compositional relative abundances, plus four cultivation covariates. Because relative-
abundance data are compositional (constrained to sum to a fixed total — the Aitchison
simplex), raw values are first transformed with a **centered log-ratio (CLR)** transform
before modeling. A regularized **XGBoost** classifier (L1 + L2 penalties, to manage the
collinearity CLR introduces) is trained to predict biomass "doubling competence," with a
decision threshold tuned toward recall on the reasoning that a missed decline is costlier
than a false alarm. **TreeSHAP** is then used to attribute each prediction back to
individual taxa in a directionally interpretable way.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | 78.9% |
| Recall | 84.4% |
| Precision | 76.0% |
| AUC-ROC | 0.91 |

*Ensifer adhaerens* — a nitrogen-fixing, cobalamin (vitamin B12)-provisioning symbiont
documented in the duckweed-microbiome literature — emerges as the single strongest
predictor in both the gain-based feature importance ranking and the SHAP summary, matching
the taxon's designed role in the synthetic generator and its independently published
biological function (see References below).

## Honest limitations (read before citing)

- **This is a synthetic dataset.** Every class-conditional effect size — including
  *Ensifer adhaerens*'s strong, clean signal — was chosen by the authors to test whether
  the pipeline could recover a designed signal, not measured from real tanks. The reported
  metrics say the *pipeline* works, not that the *biological claims* are validated.
- **The SHAP ranking of `Sphingomonas_sp`** (a taxon deliberately given no designed effect)
  can appear higher than expected — this is a documented consequence of CLR's shared
  geometric-mean term entangling every taxon's transformed value, not a hidden biological
  signal. See notebook Section 13 and the paper's Results section for the full explanation.
- **The pH/ammonium-nitrogen ranges** used in the generator are anchored to a single
  suspended-growth cultivation study, not a cross-study consensus.
- **n = 90 in the held-out test set** — precision/recall estimates at this sample size
  carry meaningful uncertainty; treat the reported percentages as approximate, not exact.

## Next steps

Real 16S rRNA amplicon-sequencing data from replicate, industrial-scale *Wolffia* tanks
would let this exact pipeline — CLR transform, regularized XGBoost, TreeSHAP attribution,
recall-tuned threshold, all unchanged — be re-run as a genuine predictive/early-warning
tool rather than a proof of concept.
